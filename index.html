01 / About

From electromagnetic design to tested RF hardware.

I earned my Ph.D. in Electronic and Information Engineering (majoring in Information and Communication Engineering) from South China University of Technology in September 2026. My research focuses on 1-bit reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, reflectarray antennas, sub-6 GHz beam steering, microwave engineering and next-generation wireless systems.

I connect full-wave electromagnetic simulation with practical hardware: ANSYS HFSS and MATLAB design, PIN-diode phase control, FPGA-based reconfiguration, antenna measurements, and wireless-link evaluation using WAPI and USRP X410. I have also worked on active RIS, Doherty power-amplifier integration, embedded systems and engineering education.

01 / Jun 2025 – Sep 2026

Southern Power Grid Active RIS Project

Developed an active RIS reflection-amplifier and 2-bit phase-shifting solution, achieving 13 dB reflection-amplifier gain and four states separated by 90°.

02 / Sep 2025 – Sep 2026

Doherty Power Amplifier–Integrated Active Antenna

Integrated Doherty power amplifier and active-antenna research. Simulations achieved 43 dBm output power, 62% peak drain efficiency and 40% efficiency at 6-dB back-off.

RIS-Based Beam-Steering Antenna

Designed and experimentally validated a 20×20 reconfigurable array with 23.1 dBi measured gain, 57% aperture efficiency and electronic beam scanning up to ±60°.
