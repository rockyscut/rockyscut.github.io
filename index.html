About Me

I am a dedicated researcher and engineering professional with extensive experience spanning academic research, university teaching, and industrial engineering.

In September 2026, I earned my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from South China University of Technology (SCUT), China, under the supervision of Professor Jie Tang (唐杰).

My doctoral research primarily focused on Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS), programmable reflectarray antennas, beam-steering technologies, and advanced wireless communication systems for 5G and 6G applications. My research experience includes the design, simulation, fabrication, and experimental validation of advanced RF and antenna systems. My professional background also encompasses university-level teaching, interdisciplinary research collaboration, and practical industrial engineering experience. I am passionate about advancing next-generation wireless technologies and translating innovative research into practical engineering solutions.

Current research affiliation: Short-Range Wireless Communications & Networks Lab, SCUT
