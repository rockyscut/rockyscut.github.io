About Me

I earned a Ph.D. in Electronic and Information Engineering (majoring in Information and Communication Engineering) from South China University of Technology (SCUT) in September 2026. My work centers on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS), sub-6 GHz reflectarray antennas, beam steering, RF and microwave systems.

My research combines full-wave electromagnetic modeling, PIN-diode switching, FPGA control, antenna prototype development and experimental validation. I also have teaching, embedded-systems research and earlier engineering-industry experience.

Current research affiliation: Short-Range Wireless Communications & Networks Lab, SCUT

Ph.D. dissertation: Design and Implementation of Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces for Beam Control

Advisor: Prof. Jie Tang, SCUT

Research interests: RIS, programmable reflectarrays, beamforming, RF front ends, active RIS, Doherty PA–antenna integration, 5G/6G wireless communications.
