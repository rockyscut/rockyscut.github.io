01 / About

From research ideas to working hardware.

I completed my PhD in Information and Communication Engineering at South China University of Technology (SCUT) in September 2026. My work focuses on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS), low-cost reflectarray antennas, RF engineering, and 5G/6G wireless communication systems.

My research combines electromagnetic design, prototype development, FPGA-based control, and experimental validation. I have worked on programmable beam steering and RIS-assisted communication links using laboratory RF platforms including the USRP-X410.

Research with measurable outcomes.

Selected hardware and wireless research themes from my work at SCUT.

PROJECT / 01

High-Gain 1-Bit Reconfigurable Reflectarray

Design and experimental characterization of a 5.1 GHz 20×20 electronically controlled reflectarray, achieving 23.1 dBi measured gain and beam-scanning operation.

5.1 GHzFPGAReflectarray
PROJECT / 02

RIS-Assisted Wireless Communication

Wireless-link experimentation with RIS-assisted propagation at 2.4 GHz, incorporating over-the-air measurements and USRP-X410 based evaluation.

2.4 GHzUSRP-X410Wireless links
PROJECT / 03

Low-Cost PIN-Diode Unit-Cell Design

Development and validation of PIN-diode controlled 1-bit reflectarray unit cells with discrete phase states for practical beam-steering applications.
